Step 3: Grammar Calibration (10-12 min)

Modals of Advice & Necessity (*Should have*, *Need to*, *Must*, *Ought to*)

To discuss past mistakes in decision making and rules for preserving energy, we use modal verbs:

1. Past Regret (*Should have*)

• We should have automated our weekly meals.

• She should have avoided checking email at night.

2. Necessity (*Need to / Must*)

• Leaders need to simplify daily choices.

• We must protect our mental bandwidth.

3. Moral Obligation (*Ought to*)

• Professionals ought to establish firm boundaries.

• Teams ought to streamline decision workflows.

Calibration Drills (4 Challenges)

1. "We ______ planned our schedule in advance instead of deciding on the spot yesterday."

2. "To prevent mental exhaustion, executives ______ streamline trivial daily choices."

3. "We ______ protect our morning cognitive bandwidth for high-value tasks."

4. "She ______ checked her emails before starting critical work this morning."
