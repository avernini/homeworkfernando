Step 3: Grammar Calibration (10-12 min)

Concession Structures (*Even though* / *Despite*) & Cause-Effect

To discuss digital habits and opposing forces, we use advanced concession phrasing (Even though... / Despite the fact that...):

1. Concession with *Even though*

• Even though we want focus, apps distract us.

• Even though it's hard, she deleted social media.

2. Concession with *Despite*

• Despite knowing the risks, we scroll daily.

• Despite constant alerts, he stayed focused.

3. Purpose with *In order to*

• We add friction in order to resist temptation.

• Apps use design tricks in order to hook users.

Calibration Drills (4 Challenges)

1. "______ knowing the risks, people spend hours on social feeds."

2. "______ we try to protect our time, algorithms easily pull us back."

3. "Tech companies engineer notifications ______ maximize daily engagement."

4. "______ the fact that notifications are distracting, most keep them enabled."
