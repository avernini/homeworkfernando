Step 3: Grammar Calibration (10-12 min)

Modals of Deduction & Future Prediction (*Will likely*, *Bound to*, *Might*)

To discuss future workplace scenarios with varying degrees of certainty, we use advanced prediction modals:

1. High Probability (*Will likely*)

• AI will likely automate routine administrative tasks.

• Soft skills will likely become the main hiring filter.

2. Certainty / Inevitability (*Bound to*)

• Change is bound to happen in every industry.

• Continuous learning is bound to define modern careers.

3. Possibility (*Might / Could*)

• Machines might eventually assist in complex ethics.

• Empathy could become the rarest corporate asset.

Calibration Drills (4 Challenges)

1. "Routine technical coding ______ be fully automated by AI in the near future."

2. "With rapid technological disruption, job restructuring is ______ happen."

3. "Emotional intelligence ______ replace pure technical knowledge in leadership roles."

4. "Lifelong learning ______ become a mandatory requirement for career survival."
