Step 1: Immersive Warm-Up (5-8 min)

The Hypothetical Client Deal

International freelancing often requires creating hypothetical scenarios to close deals. If a client wants a long-term commitment, what would you offer? Knowing how to frame conditions using hypothetical English allows you to negotiate prices and terms without sounding aggressive.

Freelance Business · Crafting Hypothetical Deals
Two Negotiation Styles
The Strategic Negotiator

This freelancer uses hypothetical conditions ("If you signed a 6-month contract, we would reduce our hourly rate"). They create win-win scenarios that secure long-term income.

The Rigid Seller

This freelancer gives flat "yes" or "no" answers without offering terms or alternatives. They frequently lose international clients due to inflexible communication.

Hypothetical Thinking:

If a major international client offered you a huge project starting tomorrow, what would you do first?

Price Flexibility:

Under what conditions would you agree to lower your standard service fee for someone?

Step 3: Grammar Focus (10-12 min)

Second Conditional for Freelance Negotiations

We use the Second Conditional to talk about hypothetical situations and their results in business negotiations. The structure combines If + Past Simple with would + base verb:

Rule 1: Proposing Hypotheses

Use If + Past Simple, followed by would + base verb to negotiate terms.

• If you signed today, we would offer a 10% discount.

• If I had more time, I would accept this project.

• If we lowered our rates, we would lose money.

Rule 2: Reversing Clause Order

You can start with would first. No comma is needed in the middle.

• We would offer a discount if you signed today.

• I would accept this project if I had more time.

• We would lose money if we lowered our rates.

Fossilized Error Guard (Second Conditional Safety)
❌ NEVER USE "WOULD" IN THE IF-CLAUSE

"If you would sign today, we would agree."

"If I would have budget, I would start."

🟢 USE PAST SIMPLE IN THE IF-CLAUSE

"If you signed today, we would agree."

"If I had the budget, I would start."
