Step 3: Grammar Calibration (10-12 min)

Diplomatic Structures & Concession (*Even though*, *Provided that*, *Would you mind if...*)

To soften design critiques and propose compromises elegantly, we use advanced diplomatic clauses:

1. Concession (*Even though*)

• Even though the client wants bright red, blue fits better.

• Even though the deadline is tight, quality remains high.

2. Condition (*Provided that*)

• We can change the font provided that we keep the hierarchy.

• Extra revisions are fine provided that budget allows.

3. Polite Inquiry (*Would you mind if...*)

• Would you mind if we explored a minimalist layout?

• Would you mind if we postponed the review to Friday?

Calibration Drills (4 Challenges)

1. "______ the client requested a colorful banner, a minimalist white space works better for luxury branding."

2. "We can add extra animation frames ______ we finalize the core wireframes today."

3. "______ we explored a dark mode version for the mobile app interface?"

4. "______ the typography is unique, it might affect legibility on small mobile screens."
